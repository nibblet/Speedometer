# CARTpath Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

CARTpath is a speedometer, map, and trip log for golf carts. It is built to work
entirely on your iPhone.

## What CARTpath uses

- **Your location.** CARTpath uses your device's location, only while the app is open,
  to show your speed, heading, trip distance, your position on the map, and to time
  sunrise/sunset for the automatic day/night appearance.
- **Trips and places you save.** Trip history (date, distance, duration, top speed, and
  route) and the places you name on the map are stored on your device.
- **Your settings.** Appearance, accent color, and map mode are stored on your device.

## What CARTpath does not do

- It does not send your location, trips, places, or any other data to us or to anyone
  else. We have no servers and never receive your data.
- It does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- It does not use your location in the background.

Maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit), which is subject to
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your control

- You can turn off location access at any time in **Settings → CARTpath → Location**.
- Delete a single trip by long-pressing it in History, or tap **CLEAR** to erase all
  trips. Places can be removed from the Map tab.
- Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

CARTpath does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including
children.
